About Us

SmartDiet is a modern platform for personalized nutrition, created to help people achieve health and balance. We specialize in diets for diabetes control, weight loss, muscle gain, and maintaining shape. Our mission is to make healthy eating accessible, simple, and effective for everyone.

Our Story

Founded in 2023 in Ukraine, SmartDiet began as an idea to help people with type 2 diabetes through personalized menus with a low glycemic index. Today, we offer a free calorie calculator, ready-made PDF menus, and advice from dietitians. In 2026, we expanded our services, adding support for various goals: from weight loss to muscle gain.

Our Team

The SmartDiet team consists of certified dietitians, endocrinologists, and developers. We collaborate with medical experts to ensure scientifically based content. Every nutrition plan is checked for safety and effectiveness.

Why Choose Us?

  • Personalization: The calorie calculator takes into account age, weight, activity, and goals.
  • Accessibility: Free tools and affordable premium plans.
  • Science: Based on recommendations from WHO, the American Diabetes Association, and Diet No. 9.
  • Community: Join us on Instagram, Telegram, and TikTok for motivation.
