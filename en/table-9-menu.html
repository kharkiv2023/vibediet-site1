Table №9: Daily Menu

Classic therapeutic diet for normalizing carbohydrate metabolism

Ready Daily Plan

Meal Menu Option
Breakfast Cottage cheese casserole without sugar (with stevia) or buckwheat porridge with 1.5% milk
Second Breakfast Unsweetened apple or a glass of kefir with bran
Lunch Vegetarian borscht or soup with meatballs + baked fish with broccoli
Afternoon Snack Rosehip decoction and 30g walnuts
Dinner Boiled chicken breast + vegetable stew (zucchini, eggplant, tomato)

Cooking Rules:

  • Methods: Only boiling, baking, or steaming. Frying is prohibited.
  • Salt: Limit to 5-6 g per day (1 teaspoon).
  • Sugar substitutes: Use stevia, erythritol, or xylitol.
  • Temperature: Dishes should be moderately warm.

Need a personalized portion?

Table №9 is a base, but calorie amount depends on your weight and goal (weight loss or maintenance).
