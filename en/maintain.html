Weight Maintenance Diet

The goal of weight maintenance is to consume as much energy as you expend. This allows you to stay active and preserve muscle mass without excess fat.

Healthy Plate Rule:

  • • 50% — Vegetables and fruits
  • • 25% — Complex carbohydrates (grains)
  • • 25% — Protein foods
  • • Healthy fats (nuts, oils)
